Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Slow Hours Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used by a single person, the owner of the YouTube channel "Slow Hours", to upload their own videos to their own channel.

Data the app accesses

The app uses Google OAuth to obtain permission to upload videos to the channel owner's YouTube channel (YouTube Data API v3). It only accesses the account of the person who authorizes it.

Data storage

The OAuth token is stored locally on the owner's computer. The app has no server, no database and no analytics, and it does not collect or store personal data on any external system.

Data sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
