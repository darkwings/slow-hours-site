Slow Hours Uploader

Slow Hours Uploader is a small personal tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel "Slow Hours" (@SlowHours.AmbientMusic) to upload their own ambient music videos to their own channel through the YouTube Data API.

It is not a public service: it has a single user, the channel owner, and it does not collect, store or share any data from other people.
